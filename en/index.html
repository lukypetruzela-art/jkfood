Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us or call +420 556 310 205.

Who do you supply?

Manufacturers of meat, bakery and dairy products, school and hospital canteens, care homes, restaurants and catering. For households we run an e-shop with smaller packs (in Czech).

Can you develop a blend to our recipe?

Yes. You describe the product, your technology and the flavour you are after, our technologists prepare a sample, you test it in your own production and we fine-tune it. Then we produce and deliver the blend regularly.

Can you pre-weigh blends for our production batch?

Yes. By arrangement we mix spice blends, starches and technological additives for your exact cutter or production batch – no weighing of ingredients, the same result every time and a dust-free workplace.

How fast do you deliver?

Within the Czech Republic we deliver with our own transport or by courier, usually within 5 working days of the order.

What packaging do you use?

Pack size and weight can be agreed to suit your consumption – for example PE or paper sacks. Every pack carries a batch number and we can add an EAN code on request.

Can you send samples?

Yes. Tell us in your enquiry which products you are interested in and we will prepare samples for you.

Do you provide technological procedures?

Yes, our combi blends for meat processing come with technological procedures, and we are happy to help when you introduce a new product.

Can I collect goods in person?

Yes, by arrangement you can collect goods directly from us at Větřkovice 243, Czech Republic.

Do you export?

Yes, we have customers abroad as well. Write to us in English and we will be happy to help.
